## The problem

A SaaS company's quoting process lived entirely in spreadsheets — sales reps were generating inconsistent quotes with no approval workflow, causing margin leakage and delayed deal cycles.

## What I built

- Salesforce CPQ product catalogue with pricing rules and discount schedules
- Multi-tier approval workflow routing quotes above threshold discount to line manager then Finance
- Custom quote PDF template replacing the previous Excel output

## Skills demonstrated

`Salesforce CPQ` `Price Rules` `Discount Schedules` `Approval Processes` `Quote Templates` `Requirements Gathering`

## Outcome

Quote turnaround reduced from 2 days to under 2 hours. Discount approval compliance reached 100% within 6 weeks of go-live.

## Artefacts in this folder

- `docs/` — solution design document and data mapping (anonymised)

## Notes

Stakeholder alignment on approval thresholds was the key challenge — ran two workshops before agreement. Would build a sandbox demo earlier in the process next time to make abstract config decisions more concrete for non-technical stakeholders.
